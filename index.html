THE WOMAN BEHIND THE WORDS

Meet Oluwapeyibomi.

For the last decade, I have been sharing my life online. What started in 2016 with putting my thoughts on a blog has now become the videos you see today on YouTube and other social media platforms.

Oluwapeyibomi is a twenty-something digital creator who is all about romanticising daily life and building a community rooted in personal growth. On my social media platforms, I share my honest, relatable lifestyle diary — from embracing my soft girl era and sharing my faith, to stepping out of my comfort zone with fun personal challenges.
